# Related Work search protocol

## Databases

IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Scopus/Web of Science (nếu có quyền truy cập),
Crossref và Google Scholar để citation chaining. GS1 standards must use primary
GS1 documentation.

## Time window and languages

- Core comparison: 2021–2026.
- Seminal work before 2021 may be included when methodologically necessary.
- English and Vietnamese records; manuscript synthesis in English.

## Search concepts

1. `agricultural traceability AND blockchain AND EPCIS`
2. `food supply chain AND GS1 EPCIS 2.0`
3. `blockchain oracle AND garbage in garbage out AND traceability`
4. `Merkle root anchoring AND supply chain`
5. `offline first AND smallholder farmer AND mobile application`
6. `super app OR mini app AND agriculture adoption`

## Inclusion criteria

- Peer-reviewed or authoritative standards source.
- Describes architecture/data model/evaluation sufficiently for comparison.
- Directly informs EPCIS, input trust, on-chain complexity or accessibility.

## Exclusion criteria

- Marketing material without technical evidence.
- Duplicate/preliminary version when a complete version exists.
- Blockchain mention without traceability implementation or evaluation.

## Extraction fields

Citation, year, commodity/context, chain type, data standard, input-validation
mechanism, on-chain complexity, offline support, identity/wallet requirement,
evaluation scale, dataset, metrics and stated limitations.

Two passes are required: title/abstract screening, then full-text eligibility.
Record exclusion reasons. The comparison table is generated only from extracted
fields, not memory.
